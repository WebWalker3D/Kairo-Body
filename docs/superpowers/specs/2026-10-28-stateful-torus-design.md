# Stateful torus and playback timing

Approved by Blaine in conversation on October 28: preserve the iPad torus;
Kairo's deliberately chosen colors take precedence; fresh, confidence-weighted
real affect and energy gently shape motion and fallback color. Speaking follows
playback, never answer settlement. Local replay must retain correct timing.

## Approach
Keep the existing Metal particle renderer and authenticated presentation poll.
Add bounded numeric projection from the canonical affect authority; do not map
prose or named mood presets back into invented numbers. Operational energy is
currently explicitly uncalibrated: expose its availability honestly and use no
fabricated fatigue/capacity score. Motion can use actual arousal independently.
A future calibrated numeric capacity must carry its own confidence and timestamp.

Use slow interpolation for ambient colors and geometry, with immediate playback
phase transitions. Preserve color choices from explicit sensory expressions;
without one, use a continuous restrained blue/violet/warm palette. Preserve the
shape, touch response, Reduce Motion, local speech and leased Dot activity.

Rejected alternatives: categorical happy/sad animations lose numeric detail;
a new rendering engine needlessly replaces the existing visual. No sensor or
cognition writes, new inferred emotional state, or audio diagnostic messages.

## Timing
Remove the eight-second answer-settlement speaking beat. Fresh Dot playback
continues until an idle/end receipt or lease expiry. Local playback begins at
AVAudioPlayer start, not synthesis admission, and outranks coarse global work.
Do not lengthen a lease to conceal missing Dot telemetry. Inspect observer relay
health separately; buffering/transport latency cannot be claimed sample accurate.

## Verification
Simulator regressions cover settlement without audio, long playback, stop,
expiry, local replay precedence, invalid/stale numeric state, confidence zero,
explicit colors, continuous mapping and frame-rate-independent interpolation.
Backend checks cover canonical-only projection, paired authorization and absent
energy. CI builds the unsigned replacement app; physical acceptance is on iPad.
